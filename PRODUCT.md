# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: single static HTML file with inline CSS/JS and local images, so it can be dropped onto any host or landing-page builder used for ads.

## Users

Marketing and brand managers, event/expo coordinators and business owners in Jakarta / Tangerang who are preparing a trade-show booth, a showroom, a pop-up or a commercial interior. They arrive cold or warm from a paid ad (Meta / Google), usually on a phone, often with an event date already fixed.

## Product Purpose

Ads landing page for Hanuman Construction (Hanuman Exhibition and Interior Construction), a design-and-build contractor operated by PT Imaji Nova Caraka. Success = a visitor submits the short project form, which hands off to the Hanuman WhatsApp line (+62 811-9118-898) with the brief prefilled.

## Positioning

One team designs and physically builds the booth or interior, in-house, since 2015. Proof is the built work: Chandra Asri, Canon (Asian Games 2018), Honda, KIA Keramik, TOTO/Surya Pertiwi, MODENA, UNIQLO, TACO, EVOPET (Best Booth Design at IIPE 2025 and 2026).

## Operating Context

Exhibitions at ICE BSD, JIExpo Kemayoran, mall atriums (Senayan City), showrooms. Booth work runs to a fixed event date; build happens in a short install window before opening.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: Exhibition Booth Design & Build; Interior Design & Construction (commercial: showroom, office, F&B); Pop Up Booth Design & Build; Event Support Design & Build. Pricing is quote-based; no public prices. Copy is Indonesian body with English display headlines (user's script).

## Brand Commitments

Logo: white rounded geometric wordmark "HANUMAN" inside a deep maroon (#841F1F) pill, with "EXHIBITION AND INTERIOR CONSTRUCTION" in tracked geometric caps below. Maroon is the brand color. Copy script provided by the user is binding in structure; wording may be tightened.

## Evidence on Hand

Real project photos downloaded from hanumanexpo.com (raw/). Client list from hanumanexpo.com. Awards: EVOPET booth, Best Booth Design at IIPE 2025 and IIPE 2026 (per the user's script). No testimonials, no project metrics, no team photos: do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Show the built thing before describing it.
- Every section ends one tap away from the form.
- A rough idea is enough to start; never make the visitor feel they need a finished design.
- Claims stay to what the script and site state.

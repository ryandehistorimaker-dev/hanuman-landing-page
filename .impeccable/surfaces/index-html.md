# Surface brief: Hanuman ads landing page (index.html)

Scope: single ads landing page. Mode: Persuade. Audience: brand/marketing managers and business owners in Jabodetabek planning a booth, showroom, pop-up or commercial interior, mostly on phones from Meta/Google ads. Action: short project form that opens WhatsApp (+62 811-9118-898) with a prefilled brief. Proof: real project photos from hanumanexpo.com, client names from the same site, the user's Selected Projects list. Constraints: user's copy script, tightened, structure kept; no invented testimonials, numbers or prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is built from the same stock as the booths: maroon HPL laminate panels with exposed plywood edges, mounted over real build photos. It refuses the dark-overlay hero with centred white headline and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Maroon HPL (#821D1D) carries big fields; plywood edge (layered tan stripes) shows wherever a panel ends; concrete-grey workshop ground; charcoal for night. Wide rounded display (Unbounded) echoing the logo, tracked Montserrat caps as sample-code labels. Depth only from stacked layers and visible edges (raise from papercut), no gradients or fake glow.

STORY: Visitor sees a real built booth, reads one line of what Hanuman does, picks their project type, sees four service samples, built work and a ruled project schedule (raise from split-flap), why one team, the process as layers stacking, then sends the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Chandra Asri booth photo. Lower-left a maroon HPL board with ply edge on top/right, overlapping into the next section: H1 at display scale (oversized word "EXPERIENCE", raise from shader), one sentence, project-type selector chips that preset the form, primary button. Logo pill top-left, WhatsApp link top-right. Mobile: photo top 45%, board below it, button visible.

FORM: Material sample board / workshop swatch. Candidate 5 of 7 on the grounded list. Seed key e2349c1f. Signature interaction: choosing a project type anywhere (hero chips, service sample CTAs) pastes that sample onto the form, preselected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
